🎯 Victory Condition

⚔️ Combat Resolution

  • Highest total strength in a region wins the combat.
  • Leadership Bonus adds +1 strength to other units in the region.
  • Fortress Defense gives +2 defense bonus to the defender.

📋 Issuing Orders
